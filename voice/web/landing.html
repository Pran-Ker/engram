Record a dataset of your voice.
Then fine-tune a model on it.

Read sentences into your mic here. Download the cleaned clips. Run one script on a GPU and Liquid's 1.5B audio model learns to speak as you.

  1. Record. Sentences appear one at a time. Space records, the words light up as you say them, Enter keeps the take. Ten minutes is the practical minimum; thirty sounds like you.
  2. Download. One zip: your clips trimmed, loudness-normalized and resampled to 24 kHz, a manifest, and finetune.py.
  3. Train. On any CUDA machine: python finetune.py train --data voice-… --name "You". Ten minutes of audio is about ten minutes on an A100. Then finetune.py say renders any sentence in your voice.

You get a link like /v/8f3a19c2/. That link is the voice: bookmark it to come back, and anyone who has it can add to it or download it. Use Chrome or Edge for the word-by-word highlighting. Nothing else is stored about you.

source, steps and finetune.py model: LiquidAI/LFM2.5-Audio-1.5B
