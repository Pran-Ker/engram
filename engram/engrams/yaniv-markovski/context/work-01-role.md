I'm Head of Ecosystem Engineering at Nimble.

Nimble Way (nimbleway.com) is an AI-powered web data platform that gives enterprises live, structured access to the public web for AI agents and decision-making workflows. Its Web Search Agents autonomously browse, verify, and extract data, returning curated tables that plug directly into data warehouses such as Databricks, Snowflake, and Microsoft Fabric. Customers include Fortune 500 banks, retailers, CPGs, hedge funds, and AI-native startups that need trustworthy, governed web data instead of brittle in-house scrapers.
