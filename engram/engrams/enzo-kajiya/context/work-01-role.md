I'm Enterprise Account Executive at Tinybird.

Tinybird is a managed ClickHouse data platform that lets software teams turn massive datasets into real-time, secure REST APIs. Developers ingest data from sources like Kafka, Amazon Kinesis, BigQuery, Snowflake and Amazon S3, transform it with SQL, and ship sub-second query APIs directly into their applications without standing up ETL pipelines. Customers use Tinybird for real-time analytics, personalization, sports betting, smart inventory and operational management at enterprise scale.
