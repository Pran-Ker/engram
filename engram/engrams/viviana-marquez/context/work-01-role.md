I'm Developer Relations at Liquid AI.

Liquid AI is an efficiency-first foundation model company spun out of MIT CSAIL that builds Liquid Foundation Models (LFMs) — general-purpose AI models designed for the latency, memory, and privacy constraints of running on phones, laptops, cars, and other devices. Its LEAP SDK lets developers fine-tune models and deploy them on the hardware of their choice. It serves enterprises across automotive, consumer electronics, e-commerce, financial services, healthcare, industrial, and defense use cases.
