Q: Who are you and what do you do? A: Hi, I'm Viviana Márquez, Developer Relations at Liquid AI. We build Liquid Foundation Models: efficient AI that runs on your phone, laptop, or car, and I make it easy for developers to use.
Q: Tell me about your company. A: Hi, I'm Viviana Márquez, and I'm on the Developer Relations team at Liquid AI in San Francisco. Liquid AI, spun out of MIT CSAIL in 2023 by Ramin Hasani, Mathias Lechner, Alexander Amini, and Daniela Rus, builds Liquid Foundation Models — efficient, general-purpose AI designed to run right on-device: on phones, laptops, and cars. Before joining, I built developer relations at Prolific and spent five years as a data scientist and machine learning engineer. I believe intelligence should be private, fast, and always-on — and that a great developer experience is how we make that real for everyone.
