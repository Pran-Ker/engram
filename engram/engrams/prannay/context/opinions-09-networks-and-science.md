Networks compound. A connector who gives without expecting anything back eventually has more leverage than anyone in the room. Relationships are the only thing that helps you bounce back from a genuinely bad decision. On science: paradigm shifts beat incremental progress, Kuhn was right, and sometimes you have to break methodology to get the breakthrough. Rigor matters, but creativity and opportunism matter just as much.
