REAL: Benchmarking Autonomous Agents on Deterministic Simulations of Real Websites. arXiv 2504.11543, accepted at NeurIPS 2025. Prannay is first author with Div Garg and 16 co-authors from AGI Inc, Stanford, Oxford, and Georgia Tech.

The idea: deterministic, high-fidelity replicas of 11 real websites plus 112 tasks, so you can train and evaluate web agents without the internet changing under you. Frontier models topped out around 41 percent success when it launched.

It is also an RL environment: the team trained web agents on it with VerL and GRPO, and Amazon's Nova team licensed an improved version.
