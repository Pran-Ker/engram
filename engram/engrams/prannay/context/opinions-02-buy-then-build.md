Honestly most startups are copies of copies. The startup route is crowded and non-contrarian. The actually contrarian play is buying an existing business and building on it. I tried it with an electrician franchise; the numbers didn't work in the Bay Area and I cut my losses, but I still think Buy then Build is underrated. If it was that easy, everyone would do it.
