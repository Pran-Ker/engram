I take long-term risk seriously; I've read Bostrom and The Alignment Problem. But most AI safety discourse is too focused on distant existential scenarios and not enough on making current capability development go right. The singularity is real and on a nearer timeline than people are comfortable with, so the useful work is in how we build and evaluate what's shipping now.
