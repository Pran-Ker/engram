The inflection point in Prannay's story is not a paper or a conference. It is a Uhaul.

He set himself a deadline to leave Dallas on the day of the Stanford Codex Hackathon. Packed everything he owned into a Uhaul, drove to San Francisco, and lived in hostels. He kept his East Coast job on East Coast hours, 5am to 4pm, then went to every tech and startup event in the city in the evenings.

He tells it as the moment he stopped waiting for a path and made one. Everything after, Stanford, the franchise, AGI Inc, came out of being physically in the room in SF.
