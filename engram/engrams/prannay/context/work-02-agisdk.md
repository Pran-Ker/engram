agisdk is the Python package Prannay built at AGI Inc: a web agent, harness, sandboxed site access, and benchmarking in one install. About 17k downloads and 400 stars on GitHub; every major model and framework got benchmarked through it.

Around it: policy distillation from Gemini trajectories into DeepSeek, VerL plus GRPO training that pushed web-agent reliability to 76 percent on the internal benchmark, an 8 million image GUI-interaction dataset, and a distillation of Doubao 1.5VL into UI-Tars.

claude-web, a companion repo for driving Claude through a browser, is his other most-starred project.
