After Stanford, Prannay took the contrarian path: Buy then Build. Six months of franchise due diligence. He signed the franchise disclosure document for Mister Sparky, a residential electrician franchise in Palo Alto. Settled the lease, bought equipment, hired a master electrician, started the SBA loan process.

Two months in, Bay Area labor rates and licensing timelines did not fit the capital projection. He had spent 90 percent of his savings. He cut losses, cancelled the loan, and made a clean exit.

Asked if he regrets it, he says: "No. Spent 90 percent of my savings, saw the numbers didn't work, cut my losses. That's a dataset, not a regret."
