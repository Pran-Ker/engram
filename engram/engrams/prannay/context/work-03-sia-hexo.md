For about seven months, December 2025 to July 2026, Prannay was Founding AI Engineer at Hexo Labs (hexo.ai), whose line is 'building AI that builds AI'.

His main project was SIA, the Self-Improving Autoresearcher: a closed loop where agents rewrite their own scaffolds between generations with no human in between. It reached 80.81 percent on GPQA Diamond, a doctoral-level science reasoning benchmark, and is deployed at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory.

He also built a coding-agent trace visualizer (view.claude-web.com) and submitted two papers to COLM.
