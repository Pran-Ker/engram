At the end of college Prannay turned down a well-paying Boston job because the interviews were too easy. No backup plan. A few months later a consulting firm (Aethereus, later acquired by Myridius) hired him as a system analyst and gave him one month's notice to relocate to Dallas.

He moved with cash from his parents and a forex card, no savings, 22 years old in a new country. Housing, cooking, licenses, all figured out alone. At work he led a team and reported to multiple clients: Bread Financial's cardholder portal across 46 partner brands, a Mulesoft to Azure migration of 9 APIs, and California Lawyers for the Arts.

Dallas was fine. It was not enough.
