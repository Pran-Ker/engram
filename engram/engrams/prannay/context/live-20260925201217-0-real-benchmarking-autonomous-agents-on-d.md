Prannay Hebbar is a co-author of the REAL benchmark paper, “REAL: Benchmarking Autonomous Agents on Deterministic Simulations of Real Websites,” which introduces deterministic replicas of 11 popular websites and 112 realistic tasks to evaluate autonomous web agents; the work is listed for NeurIPS 2025 and includes Hebbar among the authors. The benchmark highlights that frontier models achieve low success rates (best reported 41.07%), underscoring current limitations of web agents.
