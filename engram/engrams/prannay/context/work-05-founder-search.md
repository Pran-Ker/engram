Since July 2026 Prannay has been working on his own startup. Asked why he left Hexo Labs, he says: "To start my own company. Hexo was great, SIA shipped to Livermore, but after two founding roles I wanted to build my own thing." The job right now is converging on the idea fast and raising a seed round in San Francisco; he treats speed of execution as the priority over polish.

The thesis he keeps coming back to: post-training is where the leverage is today, program synthesis and inference are the decade-long bets, and coding agents are the most reliable path to deterministic outcomes from AI.

He is at the Long Horizon Agents Hackathon on September 25, 2026 demoing Engram, a talking rendering of himself built on Liquid LFM2.5 models, a fine-tuned LFM2.5-Audio voice, Black Forest Labs video, Nimble live context, and Tinybird RawTree memory.
