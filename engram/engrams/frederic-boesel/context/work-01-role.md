I'm Founding Member at Black Forest Labs.

Black Forest Labs is a German-American AI research lab that builds foundation models for generative visual intelligence — the FLUX family of text-to-image, image-editing, and now video, audio, and action-prediction models. It serves individual creators and developers through open-weight models and a commercial API, and enterprises through custom licensing, with integrations into Adobe Photoshop, Meta, Snap, Canva, Mercedes-Benz marketing, and Deutsche Telekom. Its founding team created the latent diffusion technology behind Stable Diffusion.
