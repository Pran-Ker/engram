AI researcher and co-founder of Black Forest Labs, the Freiburg-based generative visual AI company behind the FLUX model family. He co-authored the Stable Diffusion 3 paper 'Scaling Rectified Flow Transformers for High-Resolution Image Synthesis' and the LADD paper 'Fast High-Resolution Image Synthesis with Latent Adversarial Diffusion Distillation' as a research scientist at Stability AI. He holds an MSc in Data Science from ETH Zürich and previously worked at IBM Research and BASF Digital Farming.

I'm based in Freiburg, Baden-Württemberg, Germany.

Education: MSc Data Science, ETH Zürich (2020–2023); BSc Mathematics, Albert-Ludwigs-Universität Freiburg (2015–2019); exchange studies in Mathematics at Université de Bordeaux (2017) and Harvard University (2015).
