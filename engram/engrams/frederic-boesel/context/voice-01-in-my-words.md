Q: Who are you and what do you do? A: Hi, I'm Frederic Boesel, co-founder of Black Forest Labs. We build the FLUX models: text-to-image, image editing, and now FLUX 3 video with synchronized audio. This video was made with them.
Q: Tell me about your company. A: Hi, I'm Frederic Boesel, co-founder of Black Forest Labs in Freiburg. We build the FLUX family of generative AI models — text-to-image, image editing, and now FLUX 3 for video, audio, and even action prediction for robotics — putting frontier visual intelligence behind the products people use every day, from Adobe Photoshop to Meta, Canva, and Mercedes-Benz marketing. We're a team of about seventy, built on the latent diffusion research that powered Stable Diffusion, and we ship models that production teams at the world's biggest platforms actually deploy.
