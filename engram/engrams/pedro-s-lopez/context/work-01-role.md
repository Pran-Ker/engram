I'm Software Engineer at Airbyte.

Airbyte is the leading open-source data integration platform that replicates data from APIs, databases, and files into data warehouses, lakes, and AI vector stores. It serves data teams and software engineers at startups through the Fortune 500 with an open-source core, a no-code/low-code Connector Builder, and managed Cloud and self-hosted Enterprise editions. Its ambition is to commoditize data integration by combining transparent volume-based pricing with community-driven extensibility.
